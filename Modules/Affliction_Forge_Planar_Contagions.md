# Affliction Forge: Planar Contagions

**Planar Contagions** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **32 original extraplanar afflictions from levels 0 through 20**, designed for creatures, locations, and adventures touched by realities beyond the Material Plane.

The collection includes **12 diseases, 9 poisons, and 11 curses** originating from elemental forces, strange planar environments, supernatural corruption, extraplanar creatures, and metaphysical phenomena. Afflictions range from elemental infections and otherworldly venoms to curses that distort thought, time, fate, life force, and the relationship between body and soul.

Planar Contagions makes extensive use of Affliction Forge's advanced mechanics, including **stubborn and virulent progression, persistent damage, healing restrictions, blocked speech, condition locks, concentration gates, staged conditions, and a high-level death effect**. Afflictions can progress over individual combat rounds, hours, or several days, allowing them to function as immediate creature abilities as well as lingering consequences of planar exploration.

Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its **creature associations, families, habitats, themes, origins, and delivery methods**. Strong `planar` habitat and origin tagging allows **Creature Forge** to discover suitable afflictions automatically for celestials, fiends, fey, monitors, elementals, spirits, undead, aberrations, constructs, dragons, and many other creatures.

Natural and supernatural creature poisons delivered through **bites, stings, contact, spores, or innate abilities** remain creature afflictions and do not use Affliction Forge's charge-based weapon injury-poison system.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library.

All afflictions contained in **Planar Contagions** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

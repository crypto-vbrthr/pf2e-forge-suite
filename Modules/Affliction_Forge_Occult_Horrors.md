# Affliction Forge: Occult Horrors

**Occult Horrors** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **32 original occult-themed afflictions from levels 0 through 20**, created for campaigns where the things that infect body and mind are not always entirely natural.

The collection includes **13 curses, 11 diseases, and 8 poisons**, with strong themes of **dreams, mental corruption, shadows, parasites, mutation, necrotic influence, strange fungi, and supernatural contamination**. Its afflictions are particularly suited to aberrations, spirits, fey, undead, planar creatures, parasites, and other entities touched by unsettling occult forces.

Occult Horrors makes extensive use of Affliction Forge's advanced mechanics, including **stubborn and virulent progression, healing restrictions, condition locks, blocked speech, concentration gates, persistent damage, staged conditions, and other escalating consequences**. Afflictions can unfold over anything from individual combat rounds to hours or several days, allowing them to serve as immediate threats or lingering campaign complications.

Natural or supernatural venoms delivered through **bites, stings, and creature abilities** remain innate creature afflictions and do not use Affliction Forge's weapon-coating charge system.

Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its **creature associations, families, habitats, themes, origins, and delivery methods**. This allows **Creature Forge** to discover and select suitable occult afflictions automatically without requiring a direct dependency between the two modules.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library.

All afflictions contained in **Occult Horrors** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

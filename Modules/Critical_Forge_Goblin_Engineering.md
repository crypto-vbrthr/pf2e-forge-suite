# Critical Forge: Goblin Engineering

**Goblin Engineering** is a humorous content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **90 original critical-failure cards** about weapons, tools, and devices that suddenly become far more inventive than their owners ever intended.

The humor comes from **unreliable equipment, questionable safety features, unnecessary mechanisms, improvised diagnostics, sparks, squeaks, paperwork, and hardware developing opinions of its own**. The cards are designed to be amusing without making the character the joke, and no result permanently destroys equipment or creates repair costs.

The add-on contains three complete 30-card packs:

- **Weapon Malfunctions** covers critical fumbles with non-spell melee weapons. Instead of ordinary grip or stance failures, the weapon itself becomes the source of the problem through temporary safety modes, strange property changes, unexpected attachments, diagnostic systems, movement oddities, and occasional controlled benefits.
- **Ranged Engineering** focuses on non-spell ranged weapons and their increasingly ambitious hardware. Ammunition systems, reload mechanisms, sights, recoil controls, bow strings, sling components, retrieval systems, pressure regulators, and other mechanisms can all develop temporary and frequently unnecessary new behavior.
- **Equipment Incidents** adds additional consequences to critical failures with equipment-heavy skill actions such as **Repair, Craft, Pick a Lock, Disable a Device, Treat Wounds, Administer First Aid, Treat Disease, and Treat Poison**. The normal PF2e critical-failure result is always resolved first, after which Goblin Engineering adds its own short-lived complication or occasional bounded upside.

Goblin Engineering deliberately occupies a different space from other Critical Forge expansions. **Martial Consequences** focuses on stance, timing, movement, and combat rhythm, **Arsenal** on damage-type-specific physical consequences, and **Arcane Backlash** on magical failures. Goblin Engineering asks a different question: **what if the equipment itself is responsible?**

The cards range from purely narrative incidents to temporary mechanical changes, action costs, unusual weapon modes, utility effects, and mixed outcomes. Stronger consequences are deliberately limited, while harmless narrative incidents remain common enough to keep the add-on playful rather than relentlessly punitive.

Each of the three packs can be enabled or disabled independently.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Goblin Engineering** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Foundry VTT 14, Pathfinder 2E 8.1.2 or later, and PF2E Critical Forge 1.0.1-rc.6.1 or later.**

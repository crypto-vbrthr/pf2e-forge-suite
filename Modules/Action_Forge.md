# Action Forge

**Action Forge** is a fast, player-friendly action launcher for Pathfinder 2E in Foundry VTT, designed to bring commonly used **skill actions, core utility actions, and exploration activities** into one consistent workflow.

The integrated action catalog covers a broad range of PF2e activities, including **Athletics and Acrobatics maneuvers, social actions, Medicine, Thievery, Crafting, Recall Knowledge, magic-related checks, Earn Income, Aid, Escape, Seek, Sense Motive, and many others**. Actions that can use more than one skill allow the player to choose from the character's appropriate prepared statistics instead of duplicating the same action several times.

Action Forge uses PF2e's **native rolls and statistics** wherever possible. It automatically handles relevant proficiency requirements, target selection, fixed or defense-based DCs, roll visibility, and supported mechanical results while deliberately leaving situational decisions to the Game Master when automation would require guessing.

For actions whose difficulty must be adjudicated, the **GM remains authoritative over the DC**. Player requests are handed securely to an active GM, who can enter a value manually or use the integrated **DC by Level assistant** based on PF2e's standard level-based DCs and difficulty adjustments.

The prerequisite system can verify requirements such as **healer's tools, repair tools, alchemist's tools, thieves' tools, movement speeds, creature traits, proficiency ranks, and relevant target states**. Explicit PF2e exceptions are supported where they can be represented reliably, while requirements that depend on scene context or GM interpretation remain advisory or manual.

Action Forge also supports **multi-target actions that use a single shared roll**. Actions such as **Lie, Create a Diversion, Hide, Sneak, Conceal an Object, Palm an Object, and Steal** can roll once and compare that immutable result independently against several observers without exposing their hidden defenses to the player.

Common exploration activities such as **Search, Scout, Avoid Notice, Investigate, Defend, Detect Magic, Hustle, Follow the Expert, Repeat a Spell, and Sustain an Effect** can be recorded persistently on the acting character. Action Forge tracks the current activity without inventing immediate checks or effects that should only occur when the relevant situation arises during play.

Supported actions can also produce mechanical results. Examples include **Treat Wounds healing and temporary immunity, First Aid stabilization, Demoralize effects, Grapple, and Trip**, while more complex consequences remain under normal PF2e or GM control when direct automation would be unsafe or ambiguous.

Players can select targets from the **Canvas, sidebar, or Action Forge's out-of-combat target picker**, including other player characters without requiring ownership of their Actor. Hidden information and privileged changes remain protected through GM-side validation.

Action Forge is optionally compatible with **Critical Forge**. Ordinary native PF2e skill checks can be processed directly, while shared-roll actions use a dedicated bridge so critical outcomes can participate in Critical Forge without creating duplicate cards for every observer.

Frequently used actions can be marked as **favorites**, and the interface is designed to keep action selection, target selection, DC handling, and execution within a compact and consistent workflow.

Action Forge is available in **English and German**.

**Requires Foundry VTT 14 and Pathfinder 2E 8.4.0 or later.**

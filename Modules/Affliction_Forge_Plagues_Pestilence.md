# Affliction Forge: Plagues & Pestilence

**Plagues & Pestilence** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **28 original staged diseases from levels 0 through 20**, designed to bring sickness, outbreaks, parasites, environmental infections, and supernatural epidemics into Pathfinder 2E campaigns.

The collection ranges from relatively mundane illnesses spread by **animals, contaminated water, insects, wounds, spores, and harsh environments** to increasingly dangerous fungal, parasitic, primal, planar, and supernatural diseases. Individual afflictions are associated with suitable habitats and sources, making them useful both as creature abilities and as hazards encountered naturally during exploration and adventure.

Diseases use Affliction Forge's full staged progression system and can feature **onset periods, hourly or daily stages, Fortitude and Will saves, conditions, direct and periodic damage, healing restrictions, speech restrictions, pre-action checks, virulent progression, and lethal final stages**. Some diseases develop slowly over several days, while others can become dangerous much more rapidly.

Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its **creature associations, families, habitats, themes, origins, and methods of transmission**. This allows integrations such as **Creature Forge** to search for and automatically select diseases appropriate to a particular creature, environment, or concept without requiring a direct dependency between the modules.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library. Copies placed in the normal Affliction Forge world library remain fully editable for individual campaigns.

All diseases contained in **Plagues & Pestilence** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

# Creature Forge

**Creature Forge** is a flexible, rule-aware creature generator for Pathfinder 2E in Foundry VTT, designed to help Game Masters create complete NPCs and monsters without building every statistic, attack, ability, and special rule by hand.

Creatures can be generated by **level, role, creature category, subtype, size, and individual statistic profiles**. Creature Forge builds the resulting NPC from level-scaled PF2E values and can generate **attributes, Armor Class, Hit Points, Perception, saving throws, skills, movement speeds, senses, immunities, resistances, weaknesses, attacks, damage, and defensive adjustments**. Different roles provide coherent statistical profiles while individual values can still be customized when a particular creature concept requires it.

The generator also creates **special abilities and signature powers** appropriate to the creature's type and concept. Dragons, undead, constructs, elementals, plants, fungi, oozes, fiends, celestials, monitors, aberrations, giants, and many other creature families can receive thematic mechanics rather than merely different numbers. Generated abilities can include structured effects, saving throws, areas, limited-use powers, reactions, regeneration, temporary Hit Points, breath weapons, and other creature mechanics.

Creature Forge includes full **spellcasting generation**, selecting appropriate traditions, casting styles, spell ranks, DCs, and actual spells from configured PF2E compendia. Spell selection can take the creature's role, category, subtype, and thematic concepts into account, allowing generated spellcasters to receive coherent repertoires rather than arbitrary collections of magic.

Creatures can also be created as **Mythic Creatures** using the mythic monster rules from *Pathfinder War of Immortals / Krieg der Unsterblichen*. Creature Forge supports **Mythic Ambushers, Brutes, Casters, and Strikers**, including their statistical adjustments, Mythic Points, level-appropriate mythic abilities, and dedicated PF2E actions.

The integrated **Creature Editor** allows the Game Master to inspect and adjust the generated result, lock individual elements, and reroll entire sections or specific components without rebuilding the creature from scratch.

Creature Forge can optionally integrate with other parts of the Forge Suite. **Critical Forge** can provide the shared Effect Engine for mechanical abilities, **Aura Forge** and **Affliction Forge** can contribute suitable auras and afflictions, while **Item Forge** and **Loot Forge** can generate carried equipment, signature items, salvage, and treasure hoards. Generated afflictions, auras, spells, and other resources retain their source information and are materialized through the module that owns their underlying rules.

A stable **public API, embedded editor, content registry, and extension system** allow other modules to use Creature Forge as a generation engine or contribute additional creature categories, subtypes, abilities, spell profiles, auras, afflictions, and other content without duplicating the core generation logic.

All optional integrations remain modular, and Creature Forge can generate complete PF2E creatures without requiring the rest of the Forge Suite.

Creature Forge is available in **English and German**.

# Critical Forge: Martial Consequences

**Martial Consequences** is a tactical content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **120 original critical-event cards** focused on how exceptional martial attacks change the immediate flow of battle.

Rather than adding more injuries or extra damage, Martial Consequences concentrates on **stance, timing, positioning, openings, firing lanes, battlefield control, and momentum**. Its cards create short-lived tactical situations that make critical hits and critical failures feel different without overlapping heavily with the damage-type-specific effects of **Arsenal**.

The add-on contains four complete 30-card packs:

- **Martial Attack Fumbles** covers critical failures on non-spell weapon attacks, creating consequences involving lost footing, broken rhythm, exposed defenses, awkward positioning, reaction pressure, and disrupted attack sequences.
- **Ranged Mishaps** focuses specifically on critical failures with non-spell ranged attacks, including problems with **cover, sightlines, firing positions, trajectory, range estimation, counterfire, and shot rhythm**.
- **Martial Openings** triggers on critical successes with non-spell Strikes and creates tactical opportunities for **allies**, such as repositioning, pursuit, improved follow-up attacks, safer actions, disrupted formations, and temporary gaps in an opponent's defenses.
- **Combat Momentum** triggers on critical successes with non-spell melee Strikes and rewards the **attacker** with personal momentum through movement, follow-through, defensive advantages, combat maneuvers, reaction opportunities, target switching, and other short-lived tactical benefits.

Martial Consequences deliberately separates these identities. **Martial Openings** is about what allies can exploit after a successful attack, while **Combat Momentum** represents the attacker personally seizing control of the fight. **Ranged Mishaps** handles failures unique to ranged combat, and **Martial Attack Fumbles** covers broader breakdowns in martial technique.

Most cards use **manual resolution** because their effects depend on immediate movement, one-use bonuses, special reaction windows, multiple-attack-penalty changes, temporary cover or visibility changes, action sequencing, claimed spaces, stance interaction, temporary reach, and other circumstances that are better handled directly at the table. Where an effect can be represented reliably, the shared **Effect Forge Engine** is used.

All four packs have completed dedicated mechanical reviews for **balance, redundancy, timing, rules clarity, reliability, and thematic overlap**.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Martial Consequences** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires PF2E Critical Forge 0.9.0-dev or later.**

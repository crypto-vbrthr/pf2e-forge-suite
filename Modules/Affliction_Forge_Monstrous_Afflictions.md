# Affliction Forge: Monstrous Afflictions

**Monstrous Afflictions** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **32 original creature-themed afflictions from levels 0 through 20**, designed specifically for monsters, supernatural beings, and other dangerous inhabitants of a Pathfinder 2E world.

The collection includes **16 diseases, 8 poisons, and 8 curses**, ranging from relatively mundane parasites and animal-borne infections to supernatural corruption, fiendish maladies, draconic toxins, extraplanar curses, and truly dangerous high-level afflictions. Their sources include **animals, beasts, fungi, plants, oozes, undead, dragons, fey, spirits, aberrations, fiends, celestials, constructs, elementals, and monitors**.

Each affliction uses Affliction Forge's staged progression system and can combine **saving throws, onset periods, stage durations, damage, persistent damage, conditions, restrictions, virulent or stubborn progression, and other supported mechanical effects**.

Monstrous Afflictions is particularly designed to work with **Creature Forge**. Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its **creature type, family, habitat, theme, origin, and delivery method**, allowing Creature Forge to discover and select suitable afflictions automatically without requiring a hard dependency between the two modules.

Natural venoms delivered through **bites, stings, claws, or similar creature abilities** remain creature afflictions rather than being treated as weapon coatings, preserving the distinction between a monster's innate poison and Affliction Forge's charge-based injury-poison system.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are provided through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library.

All afflictions contained in **Monstrous Afflictions** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

# Affliction Forge: Urban Horrors

**Urban Horrors** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **32 original urban afflictions from levels 0 through 20**, created for adventures in crowded streets, decaying districts, sewers, graveyards, docks, guild halls, cult hideouts, palaces, workshops, and the darker corners of city life.

The collection includes **12 diseases, 8 poisons, and 12 curses**. Its themes range from contaminated water, vermin, industrial pollution, and criminal toxins to haunted districts, occult conspiracies, supernatural corruption, and curses born from the secrets cities accumulate over centuries.

Urban Horrors makes extensive use of Affliction Forge's advanced mechanics, including **persistent damage, virulent and stubborn progression, concentration gates, blocked speech, condition locks, healing restrictions, staged conditions, and a level-20 death effect**. Six entries are true **weapon injury poisons** and integrate directly with Affliction Forge's existing coating and charge workflow.

Every affliction carries the `urban` habitat tag together with more specific semantic information describing its **creature associations, families, themes, origins, and delivery methods**. This allows **Creature Forge** to automatically discover appropriate afflictions for humanoids, undead, spirits, fungi, oozes, aberrations, constructs, celestials, fiends, fey, and other creatures associated with urban environments.

More specific creature matching includes families such as **rats, snakes, fish, and parasites**, making the library useful not only for humanoid intrigue and city hazards but also for monsters that thrive beneath, between, and behind the walls of civilization.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library.

All afflictions contained in **Urban Horrors** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

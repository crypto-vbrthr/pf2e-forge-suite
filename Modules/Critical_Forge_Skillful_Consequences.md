# Critical Forge: Skillful Consequences

**Skillful Consequences** is an action-focused content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **218 original critical-event cards** for Pathfinder 2E skill actions.

The add-on is designed around a simple principle: **the normal PF2e critical success or critical failure is always resolved first**. Skillful Consequences never replaces the action's existing result. Instead, its cards add a short **tactical, situational, or narrative consequence** that gives exceptional skill checks more personality and makes repeated actions feel less mechanical.

The collection currently supports **50 different PF2e actions** and is divided into four independently configurable packs:

- **Physical Actions** contains 56 cards for Athletics and Acrobatics, including Grapple, Trip, Shove, Reposition, Disarm, Climb, Swim, Tumble Through, Balance, Force Open, and others.
- **Social Actions** contains 50 cards for Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, and Performance, including Feint, Lie, Impersonate, Gather Information, Demoralize, Coerce, Request, and Perform.
- **Subterfuge Actions** contains 38 cards for Stealth, Thievery, and forgery, including Hide, Sneak, Conceal an Object, Pick a Lock, Disable a Device, Palm an Object, Steal, and Create Forgery.
- **Knowledge & Utility** contains 74 cards covering Medicine, Crafting, knowledge, exploration, and general utility actions such as Treat Wounds, Recall Knowledge, Identify Magic, Track, Sense Direction, Aid, Command an Animal, Learn a Spell, and Earn Income.

Card density is deliberately based on how often an action is expected to occur and how much meaningful consequence space remains after its normal PF2e result. Frequently used actions can receive larger mini-decks, while narrow actions use smaller selections rather than being padded with repetitive effects.

Many cards provide **small temporary modifiers, follow-up opportunities, movement options, tactical openings, information, or purely narrative consequences**. The goal is variety and memorable follow-through, not to create a second rules layer that overwhelms the original skill action.

Special care is given to **secret checks**. Actions such as Hide, Sneak, Lie, Gather Information, Recall Knowledge, Identify Magic, Sense Direction, and Create Forgery use dedicated GM-facing metadata so their cards do not need to reveal hidden outcomes or expose incorrect information to the players. Using Critical Forge's **GM Blind or GM Only** visibility is recommended when these actions are in play.

Each of the four packs can be enabled or disabled independently, allowing Game Masters to use only the categories that fit their campaign.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Skillful Consequences** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires PF2E Critical Forge 1.0.1-rc.6.1 or later, Pathfinder 2E 8.1.2 or later, and Foundry VTT 14.**

# Critical Forge: Grim Consequences

**Grim Consequences** is a serious content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **120 original critical-event cards** for darker, more dramatic campaigns.

Its central theme is the brief loss of control that follows a devastating critical outcome. Rather than focusing on slapstick, permanent injuries, destroyed equipment, or long-term character punishment, Grim Consequences creates **short but severe tactical complications** such as broken rhythm, shock, impaired coordination, defensive collapse, hesitation, overexertion, and momentary loss of combat options.

The add-on contains four complete 30-card decks:

- **Attack Consequences** for ordinary critical Strikes
- **Fortitude Consequences** for critically failed Fortitude saves
- **Reflex Consequences** for critically failed Reflex saves
- **Will Consequences** for critically failed Will saves

Each deck contains a deliberate mixture of **Light, Moderate, and Strong** outcomes, with stronger consequences weighted less frequently. The individual decks can be enabled or disabled independently.

The save-based decks intentionally exclude **spell effects**, leaving magical critical-save repercussions to **Arcane Backlash**. Will consequences additionally exclude mindless creatures where effects involving doubt, attention, resolve, memory, or deliberate choice would not make sense.

Many cards use the shared **Effect Forge Engine** for automated mechanical consequences, while more situational results rely on Critical Forge's normal GM-controlled resolution. Effects include carefully bounded penalties, action sequencing restrictions, reaction pressure, movement complications, maneuver vulnerabilities, recovery costs, temporary conditions, and tactical choices rather than simple additional damage.

Grim Consequences is designed to complement other Critical Forge expansions rather than duplicate them. **Arsenal** focuses on damage-type-specific physical consequences, **Martial Consequences** on battlefield flow and martial mishaps, **Arcane Backlash** on magical instability, and **Against All Odds** on heroic reversals under extreme pressure.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Grim Consequences** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires PF2E Critical Forge 1.0.0-rc or later.**

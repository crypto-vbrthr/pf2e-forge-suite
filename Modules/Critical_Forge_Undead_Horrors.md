# Critical Forge: Undead Horrors

**Undead Horrors** is an undead-themed content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **90 original critical-event outcomes** for encounters with the restless dead.

Rather than treating undead combat like any other battle, the add-on introduces consequences built around **unnatural animation, decaying bodies, shattered bones, spectral instability, necromantic energy, lost memories, mindless impulses, and the strange forces that hold undead creatures together**.

The add-on contains three independently configurable 30-card packs:

- **Critical Hits by Undead** provides special consequences when an undead creature scores a critical hit.
- **Critical Fumbles by Undead** gives undead attackers distinctive failures when their attacks go catastrophically wrong.
- **Critical Hits against Undead** provides specialized consequences when heroes critically strike undead creatures.

Each semantic outcome supports both **weapon and spell attacks**, resulting in **180 internal Critical Forge card definitions** representing the 90 distinct outcomes.

Cards use detailed trait-aware filtering to distinguish between **corporeal and incorporeal undead, intelligent and mindless undead, and appropriate living targets**. Results can therefore reflect what kind of undead creature is actually involved rather than drawing from a completely generic horror table.

Critical hits against undead also include outcomes that react specifically to **bludgeoning, slashing, and piercing damage**. A crushing blow might collapse an undead creature's physical structure, a slashing strike can tear apart joints or limbs, while a piercing attack might expose the necromantic force sustaining it.

Mechanical results include effects such as **temporary conditions, penalties to attacks or defenses, impaired movement, exposed weaknesses, disrupted reactions, loss of balance, and instability in an undead creature's animating force**. Incorporeal undead receive their own spectral consequences involving spiritual fractures, forced manifestation, and weakened connection to the boundary between worlds.

Most effects that can be represented safely are applied through the shared **Effect Forge Engine**. Results requiring target-specific predicates, next-roll-only modifiers, reaction suppression, Step restrictions, or similarly contextual mechanics remain manual or partially automated rather than approximating their behavior incorrectly.

All three packs can be enabled or disabled independently.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Undead Horrors** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Foundry VTT 14, Pathfinder 2E 8.1.2 or later, and PF2E Critical Forge 1.0.1-rc.6.1 or later.**

# Critical Forge: Awkward Heroics

**Awkward Heroics** is a dryly humorous content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **120 original critical-event cards** for attacks, spell attacks, and saving throws.

Its humor comes from **awkward competence, overconfidence, tactical embarrassment, physical understatement, magical mishaps, stubborn resilience, and excessively professional descriptions of very immediate problems**. The goal is to make exceptional moments amusing without turning Pathfinder into slapstick or making the characters themselves the punchline.

The collection covers ten different critical-result categories with 12 cards each:

- **Critical Attack Hits**
- **Critical Attack Fumbles**
- **Critical Spell-Attack Hits**
- **Critical Spell-Attack Fumbles**
- **Fortitude Critical Successes**
- **Fortitude Critical Failures**
- **Reflex Critical Successes**
- **Reflex Critical Failures**
- **Will Critical Successes**
- **Will Critical Failures**

Unlike a purely narrative comedy pack, **every card includes a self-contained mechanical effect**. The humor can therefore be ignored entirely and the card will still function as a legitimate Critical Forge result.

Effects are intentionally short-lived and use ordinary PF2e and **Effect Forge** mechanics such as **Off-Guard, Clumsy, Enfeebled, Frightened, Sickened, Slowed, Stupefied, Prone, Dazzled, circumstance modifiers, temporary Hit Points, Fast Healing, movement adjustments, resistances, weaknesses, and temporary immunities**.

Awkward Heroics deliberately avoids permanent injuries, destroyed equipment, additional bookkeeping, or comedy that requires the game world to stop taking itself seriously. There are no pies, banana peels, spontaneous costume failures, or reality-breaking joke mechanics hiding behind the curtain.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Awkward Heroics** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Foundry VTT 14, Pathfinder 2E 8.1.2 or later, and PF2E Critical Forge 1.0.1-rc.6.1 or later.**

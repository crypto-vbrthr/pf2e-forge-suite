# Affliction Forge: Venoms & Toxins

**Venoms & Toxins** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing 32 original staged poisons from levels 0 through 20, covering both natural creature venoms and deliberately manufactured toxins.

The collection includes venoms from spiders, snakes, scorpions, fish, insects, amphibians, beasts, and planar predators, alongside alchemical contact poisons, ingested toxins, inhaled poisons, and weapon coatings. Seven entries are dedicated injury poisons and integrate directly with Affliction Forge's charge-aware weapon and attack workflow.

Its afflictions use the full staged progression system of **Affliction Forge** and can feature Fortitude or selected Will saves, onset periods, maximum durations, poison damage, persistent damage, conditions, virulent progression, and even a high-level death effect.

A deliberate distinction is maintained between innate creature venoms and consumable weapon poisons. Venoms delivered naturally through bites, stings, or similar attacks remain creature afflictions, while specifically formulated alchemical coatings use **Affliction Forge's** injury-poison system with charges and combat-triggered application.

Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its creature associations, families, habitats, themes, origins, and delivery methods. This allows **Creature Forge** and other modules to discover and select appropriate poisons without depending on **Venoms & Toxins** directly.

All entries are available in English and German and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library. Copies transferred into the normal **Affliction Forge** world library remain independent and editable.

All poisons contained in **Venoms & Toxins** are original homebrew content created specifically for the **Forge Suite**.

Requires **Affliction Forge** 0.1.63 or later.

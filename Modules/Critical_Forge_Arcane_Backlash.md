# Critical Forge: Arcane Backlash

**Arcane Backlash** is a content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **120 original magical critical-event cards** for moments when spellcasting succeeds spectacularly, fails catastrophically, or leaves strange resonance behind.

Despite its name, Arcane Backlash is not limited to the arcane tradition. Its cards are designed for **arcane, divine, occult, and primal magic**, treating “arcane” as a broader theme of unstable and extraordinary magical phenomena.

The add-on contains four complete 30-card packs:

- **Miscast Repercussions** triggers when a **spell attack critically fails**, creating magical recoil, disrupted casting, warped space, unstable residue, exposed magical signatures, and other consequences of a spell going spectacularly wrong.
- **Defiant Reversals** triggers when a creature **critically succeeds at a saving throw against a spell**, rewarding the resisting creature with opportunities to turn the spell's magic back against the situation.
- **Spell Attack Surges** triggers when a **spell attack critically succeeds**, producing beneficial magical resonance such as improved defenses, altered range or spell geometry, exposed weaknesses, protected casting, magical insight, reaction suppression, or opportunities to Sustain and manipulate ongoing magic.
- **Overwhelming Resonance** triggers when a creature **critically fails a saving throw against a spell**, leaving it temporarily saturated with unstable magic that can interfere with defenses, movement, reactions, perception, communication, spell resistance, and other tactical options.

Arcane Backlash deliberately avoids simply adding more damage to exceptional spell results. Instead, its cards focus on **short-lived tactical consequences, magical aftereffects, altered positioning, spell interaction, battlefield geometry, information, and unusual opportunities** that make critical magical moments feel distinctive.

Where a consequence can be represented safely through the **Effect Forge Engine**, it can be applied directly. More situational cards remain intentionally manual when they depend on the original caster, battlefield geometry, ally selection, spell identity, reaction timing, forced movement, area placement, Sustain actions, conditional consumption, or other circumstances that require Game Master adjudication.

All four packs have undergone dedicated mechanical reviews to ensure that their effects remain bounded, useful, and appropriate to their triggering situations.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Arcane Backlash** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires PF2E Critical Forge 0.9.3-dev or later.**

# Critical Forge: Against All Odds

**Against All Odds** is a context-sensitive content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **480 original critical-success cards** built around the moments when heroes triumph despite being in serious danger.

Rather than reacting only to the roll itself, Against All Odds evaluates the circumstances surrounding it. The add-on extends Critical Forge's runtime context with information about **current Hit Points, nearby threats, opponent level and size, battlefield pressure, and combinations of dangerous conditions**, allowing special cards to appear only when the fiction actually matches them.

The expansion is divided into four complete themes, each containing separate **Attack, Fortitude, Reflex, and Will decks** with 30 cards per deck:

- **Bloodied Triumphs** rewards critical successes achieved while the acting character is badly wounded.
- **Surrounded, Still Standing** focuses on heroes succeeding while threatened by multiple enemies in melee.
- **Giant-Slayer Moments** celebrates critical successes against opponents substantially more powerful than the character.
- **Narrow Escapes** recognizes moments where several dangers converge at once, using an additive danger model that can take wounds, superior enemies, surrounding foes, and particularly dangerous incoming effects into account.

The thresholds used by these themes are configurable. By default, Bloodied Triumphs considers a character bloodied at **50% Hit Points or less**, Surrounded requires **two threatening enemies**, Giant-Slayer requires an opponent at least **three levels higher**, and Narrow Escapes requires a sufficient combination of danger factors.

Of the 480 cards, **407 use automated Effect Forge effects**, while **73 provide explicit manual tactical results** for situations that are better resolved through normal PF2e actions or Game Master adjudication. Effects and targeting are handled through Critical Forge's existing Effect Engine, card-selection, diagnostics, and application systems.

Against All Odds uses only the public **Critical Forge extension API**. It does not replace or patch Critical Forge internals, and all of its contextual information remains available to Diagnostics and the visual Critical Card Editor.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Against All Odds** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Critical Forge 1.0.0-rc or later.**

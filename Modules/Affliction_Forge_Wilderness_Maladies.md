## Affliction Forge: Wilderness Maladies

**Wilderness Maladies** is a content add-on for **Affliction Forge** containing **32 original wilderness-themed afflictions from levels 0 through 20**, designed for exploration, dangerous environments, wild creatures, and adventures far from the relative safety of civilization.

The collection includes **diseases, natural poisons, and primal wilderness curses** associated with a broad range of environments, including **aquatic regions, arctic wilderness, coasts, deserts, forests, jungles, mountains, plains, swamps, underground environments, and volcanic regions**.

Its afflictions use Affliction Forge's staged progression system and can feature advanced mechanics such as **stubborn and virulent progression, persistent damage, healing restrictions, blocked speech, condition locks, concentration gates, and high-level lethal effects**. Delivery methods include **bites, stings, inhalation, contact, injury, breath attacks, auras, and creature abilities**.

Natural creature poisons are deliberately distinguished from manufactured weapon poisons. Venoms delivered through **bites, stings, and innate creature abilities** remain natural afflictions and do not use Affliction Forge's charge-based weapon injury-poison system.

Every entry includes standardized semantic tags describing its **creature associations, families, habitats, themes, origins, and delivery methods**. Wilderness Maladies places particular emphasis on habitat information, allowing **Creature Forge** to combine creature identity with environment and automatically select afflictions appropriate to creatures from arctic wastes, jungles, swamps, deserts, coasts, volcanic regions, and other wilderness habitats.

All entries are available in **English and German** and are distributed through a managed, read-only Affliction Forge library.

All afflictions contained in **Wilderness Maladies** are original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires Affliction Forge 0.1.63 or later.**

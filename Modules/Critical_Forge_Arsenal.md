# Critical Forge: Arsenal

**Arsenal** is a content add-on for **Critical Forge** containing **180 original weapon critical-event cards**, expanding critical hits and critical fumbles with consequences tailored specifically to the physical character of different damage types.

The add-on contains six independent 30-card packs:

- **Slashing Critical Hits**
- **Piercing Critical Hits**
- **Bludgeoning Critical Hits**
- **Slashing Critical Fumbles**
- **Piercing Critical Fumbles**
- **Bludgeoning Critical Fumbles**

Each damage type has its own mechanical and narrative identity. **Slashing** focuses on bleeding, torn defenses, disrupted movement, and sweeping follow-through. **Piercing** emphasizes precision, exposed gaps, impaired joints, and targeted disadvantages. **Bludgeoning** revolves around balance, momentum, knockback, disorientation, lost footing, and broken combat rhythm.

Cards range from relatively light tactical complications to stronger highlight moments and can include **conditions, circumstance modifiers, movement penalties, weaknesses, persistent damage, positional effects, reaction-related consequences, and short-lived vulnerabilities or advantages**.

Arsenal is deliberately designed to make critical results more interesting without turning every critical hit into an automatic victory or every critical fumble into a lost turn. Effects are generally short-lived and bounded, with stronger outcomes appearing less frequently. Cards also use creature-trait filters where necessary, preventing results such as bleeding wounds or anatomical injuries from being applied to creatures for which they would make little sense.

Critical-hit effects normally affect the target, while critical-fumble effects generally fall back on the attacker. Mechanical consequences are handled through the shared **Effect Forge Engine**, allowing suitable cards to be applied directly from Critical Forge.

Each of the six packs can be enabled or disabled independently, allowing Game Masters to use only the damage types and result categories they want in their campaign.

All cards are available in **English and German**.

All content contained in **Arsenal** is original homebrew content created specifically for the Forge Suite.

**Requires PF2E Critical Forge 0.9.0-dev or later.**

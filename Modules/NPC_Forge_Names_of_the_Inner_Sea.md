# NPC Forge: Names of the Inner Sea

**Names of the Inner Sea** is a cultural name expansion for **PF2E NPC Forge**, adding a large collection of setting-inspired naming traditions for characters from across Golarion's Inner Sea region.

The add-on contains **53 naming cultures and 85 name packs**, including broad coverage for all **16 core ancestries supported by NPC Forge**. Human characters can draw from **46 automatically resolved cultural traditions**, while numerous ancestries also receive explicit regional variants for places and peoples such as **Kyonin, the Five Kings Mountains, Belkzen, Absalom, the Mordant Spire, Dongun Hold, Mwangi, Katapesh, Qadira, the Shackles, Osirion, Nex, Ustalav**, and many others.

Names are not limited to simple fixed lists. Many cultures and ancestries use dedicated **component-based generators, regional naming structures, family-name patterns, and optional epithets**, allowing the same cultural identity to produce a much wider variety of believable NPC names.

The library currently provides more than **190,000 theoretical base naming combinations**, with over **185,000 distinct rendered names** before optional epithets are added. Generation remains deterministic when NPC Forge uses a fixed seed, making results reproducible when needed.

Regional ancestry variants are applied deliberately rather than replacing the normal ancestry-wide naming pools. A dwarf, elf, tengu, kholo, ysoki, leshy, iruxi, or other nonhuman ancestry therefore continues to use its universal naming tradition unless a specific regional culture is selected or supplied by another module.

All content is available in **English and German**, including localized culture names, generated semantic family-name components, and epithets.

The add-on contains no independent NPC-generation logic. It registers its cultures and name packs through the public **NPC Forge cultural-name API**, leaving NPC Forge responsible for name selection, fallback behavior, deterministic generation, and NPC creation.

**Requires PF2E NPC Forge 1.1.2 or later.**
